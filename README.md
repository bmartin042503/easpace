---

**easpace** is a cross-platform, open-source application designed to be a personal mental health haven. In a fast-paced world, finding a moment of peace can be difficult. This app provides a quiet, secure space to reflect, track your well-being, and find your center.

Mental health tools should be accessible and completely safe. That is why easpace is **100% open-source and free forever**—no ads, no subscriptions, no hidden paywalls. **All your data is stored strictly locally on your device**—no servers and no cloud tracking. An encryption feature is also planned to ensure your personal thoughts remain entirely yours.

### Features

- **Journaling:** Write down your thoughts, clear your mind, and safely store your daily reflections.
- **Mood Tracking:** Keep a pulse on your emotional state and recognize your personal patterns over time.
- **Activity Tracking:** Monitor your habits, routines, and milestones with flexible tracking tools.
- **Wellness:** Step away from the noise with immersive, full-screen breathing exercises, and meditate to calming audio.

### Current Status: v0.1.0 (Work in Progress)

**easpace is currently in its very early stages of active development (v0.1.0).**
The core architecture is being built, and while the foundation is coming together, the application is not yet ready for production use.

Expect bugs, missing features, and rapid changes.
